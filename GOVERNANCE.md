# Governance

## Model

Single maintainer, for now. The framework has one author who makes final decisions on content, scope, and releases. This is stated plainly rather than dressed up: the project's value rests on a consistent editorial voice and a consistent methodology, and a committee would dilute both at this stage.

## How decisions are made and recorded

- Scope is fixed by the four-module specification in [README.md](README.md); changes to it are rare and deliberate.
- Every judgment call is recorded in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md): the decision, the alternatives, and why.
- Disagreements with a pattern are argued in public — issues and PRs — so the record is durable and attributable.

## Path to shared governance

If the project grows sustained contributors, governance widens in this order: named reviewers with acknowledged authority over specific modules, then a documented maintainer group. Reviewers who leave substantive public review are credited in acknowledgments.

## Conduct

The [Contributor Covenant](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies everywhere the project convenes.
