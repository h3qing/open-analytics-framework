# References

Bibliography for the framework. Every citation key used anywhere in `docs/`, `reference-architectures/`, or `implementation-guides/` must resolve to an entry here; CI enforces this.

## Conventions

- Keys are stable and bracketed: `[SAMBASIVAN-2021]`, `[KIMBALL-2013]` — author or institution, then year.
- Each entry: key, full reference, link where one exists.
- No entry is added without verification. Anything unconfirmed carries `[TODO: verify]` instead of a plausible-looking reference.
